# Single-Stock Deep Research · v3.3.0

[中文 README](./README.md)

This skill is for a **given company/ticker or a small explicit list**. It performs complete company research instead of scanning the market or ranking candidates.

## v3.3.0 highlights

- Enterprise-type routing: mature compounder, high-growth profitable, cyclical, early commercialization, transition/strategic asset, or mixed.
- Transition / Strategic Asset branch: SOTP, Growth vs Maintenance CapEx, utilization, normalized earnings, State Tree, Real Option, Transaction Anchors, Model Conflict Review and milestones.
- Positioning / Crowding / Squeeze layer: short crowding, long crowding, borrow stress, float/liquidity, delayed 13F/ETF proxies, options amplification, supply events, squeeze/unwind stage, trigger and invalidation.
- Strictly separates Short Interest from daily Short Volume.
- Treats high SI as Fuel rather than an active squeeze.
- Treats GEX as a model, not a dealer book.
- Keeps crowding as path/execution risk; it does not automatically alter intrinsic value.

Copy the entire directory when installing; references and assets are required parts of the skill.
